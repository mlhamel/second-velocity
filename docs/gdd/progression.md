# Progression

[active]

From shuttle to capital ship — the EV arc.

- Start: basic shuttle, a few credits, big ambitions
- Mid: light freighter / escort / mercenary hulls; weapon and shield upgrades
- Late: heavy freighters, warships, the Kestrel-class dream
- Money is the XP: trading and missions must both stay viable

Implemented per ADR-0014: ports sell hulls at shipyards and outfits at
outfitters; hull trade-in and outfit resale are 50% of base price. Death keeps
the hull, equipment, credits, and completed quests; loses cargo and active
quests; the same hull is recommissioned after the recovery delay.
